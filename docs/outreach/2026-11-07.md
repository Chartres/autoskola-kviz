## Proposal for Pavol: an open licence for the question explanations (needed for NPI OVZ)
**Decided 2026-11-07: no licence change** (Pavol, 12:45 UTC, on the decision page). There is no NPI nomination; test B continues through the other routes. Kept below as the record of what was shown.

The NPI nomination system for open educational resources takes only openly licensed material.

- **What it would be:** a licence such as CC BY 4.0 on the explanations (`data/explanations.json`) and the app's text. The official questions stay under the Ministry's terms, and the law quotes are public law.
- **What it would allow:** a nomination to the national RVP.CZ portal used by teachers.
- **Cost:** others may reuse the explanations, competitors included.

This changes what the product is, so it is your call. I took no action.
